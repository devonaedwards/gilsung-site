申 (shen), the ninth earthly branch

申 is the monkey branch, the start of autumn and the mid afternoon, where metal first shows up in the year.

申 is Metal, yang by position, monkey by animal. Its hours are 15:00 to 17:00 and its month begins at 立秋 (liqiu) in early August, which is the calendar declaring autumn while the weather is still hot.

Its hidden stems are 庚 (geng, yang Metal), 壬 (ren, yang Water) and 戊 (wu, yang Earth). Metal generating water is the standard cycle at work: autumn is where the year starts making the winter it will need.

On a 申 day the branch is Metal, and if the stem above it is 庚 or 壬 you get 庚申 or 壬申 (ren shen), two of the more unmixed pillars in the sixty.
