雨水 (yushui), rain water

雨水 is the term where winter's precipitation is expected to fall as rain instead of snow, around the nineteenth of February.

It is the second solar term and a medial one, 中氣 (zhong qi), meaning it falls inside a month pillar rather than starting one. It sits in the middle of the 寅 (yin) month that 立春 (lichun) opened.

The twenty-four terms alternate strictly, sectional then medial, all the way around the year. Only the twelve sectional ones move the month pillar, so a medial term like 雨水 is a marker of where you are inside a month rather than a change of pillar.

When the page names 雨水 as the governing term, the month pillar has not moved since 立春 and will not move until 惊蛰 (jingzhe).
