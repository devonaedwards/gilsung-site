壬 (ren), the ninth heavenly stem

壬 is yang Water: the sea and the river in flood, water with somewhere to be.

壬 is the ninth stem, Water in its yang form. The images are large bodies of moving water, which is why classical texts associate it with scale, momentum and a certain amount of not being easily stopped. Water is the element the tradition links with intelligence and with flow.

It is a yang stem and takes only yang branches: 壬子 (ren zi), 壬申 (ren shen), 壬戌 (ren xu). It is also the hidden stem inside 亥 (hai), the branch that opens winter, so 壬亥 pairings show up in the calendar's coldest stretch by design.

A 壬 day shows Water and yang on the page. It is the second-to-last stem, which means the sixty-day cycle is close to turning over whenever you see it.
