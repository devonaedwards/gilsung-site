Today's day pillar is 壬午 (ren wu), a yang Water day in the sixty-day cycle. The month pillar is 戊戌 (wu xu), governed by the term 寒露 (hanlu).

午 (wu), the seventh earthly branch

午 is the horse branch, midday and midsummer, the hottest point the twelve reach.

午 is Fire, yang by position, horse by animal. Its hours are 11:00 to 13:00 and its month opens at 芒种 (mangzhong) in early June, with the summer solstice inside it. It sits directly opposite 子 (zi) in the circle of twelve, midday against midnight.

Its hidden stems are 丁 (ding, yin Fire) and 己 (ji, yin Earth), so like 巳 (si) it reverses its outer polarity on the inside. And the same pinyin trap applies here as with the stem 戊: 午 and 戊 both write as wu without tones, which is why the pillar 戊午 looks like a stutter and is not.

A 午 day is a horse day. If you were born in a horse year, a horse day is not your year returning, only the same animal counted on a faster wheel.
