The short version

Gilsung has no developer server, no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no tracker. It makes no network call to us. What Gilsung is

Gilsung calculates and displays Four Pillars of Destiny content, known as BaZi 八字, saju 사주 and Shichuu Suimei 四柱推命: the four pillars, the ten gods, the luck pillars, the annual pillar, pairing readings and date-selection guidance. Gilsung prescribes no remedies, talismans, charms or rituals, makes no claim to cure, prevent or guarantee any outcome, and never issues a pass-or-fail verdict on a person or a pairing.

The app processes only what you choose to enter or create. The exact set depends on the features you use. All of it is processed on your device.
